=========================================
FOREIGN KEYS & RELATIONSHIPS
=========================================

1. What is a Foreign Key?

A Foreign Key is a column in one table that references the Primary Key of another table. It creates a relationship between two tables.

-----------------------------------------
2. Why are Foreign Keys Important?
-----------------------------------------

- Connect database tables.
- Maintain data integrity.
- Prevent invalid references.
- Reduce duplicate data.
- Make JOIN operations possible.

-----------------------------------------
3. Primary Key vs Foreign Key
-----------------------------------------

Primary Key:
- Uniquely identifies a record.
- Exists in its own table.

Foreign Key:
- References the Primary Key of another table.
- Creates a relationship between tables.
