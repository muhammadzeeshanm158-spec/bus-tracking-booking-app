=========================================
ONE-TO-MANY (1:N) RELATIONSHIP
=========================================

1. What is a One-to-Many Relationship?

A One-to-Many (1:N) Relationship means one record in Table A can be related to many records in Table B, but each record in Table B belongs to only one record in Table A.

-----------------------------------------
2. Why is One-to-Many Relationship Important?
-----------------------------------------

- It models real-world business scenarios.
- It reduces duplicate data.
- It improves database organization.
- It makes reporting easier.

-----------------------------------------
3. Real-Life Example
-----------------------------------------

One Customer

↓

Many Orders

Each customer can place many orders, but each order belongs to only one customer.

-----------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------
5. Advantages
-----------------------------------------

- Better data organization.
- Easy data retrieval.
- Reduced duplication.
- Improved scalability.

-----------------------------------------
6. Conclusion
-----------------------------------------

One-to-Many is the most common relationship used in relational databases because it accurately represents many real-world scenarios.
