=========================================
ENTITY RELATIONSHIP (ER) DIAGRAM
=========================================

1. What is an ER Diagram?

An Entity Relationship (ER) Diagram is a visual representation of database tables (entities) and the relationships between them.

-----------------------------------------
2. Why is an ER Diagram Important?
-----------------------------------------

- Shows all database tables.
- Shows relationships between tables.
- Helps developers understand the database.
- Reduces design mistakes.
- Acts as a blueprint before development.
