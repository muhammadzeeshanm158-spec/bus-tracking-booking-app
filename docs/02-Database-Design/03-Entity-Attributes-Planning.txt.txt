=========================================
ENTITY ATTRIBUTES PLANNING
=========================================

1. What are Attributes?

Attributes are the properties or characteristics of an entity. In a database table, attributes become columns.

-----------------------------------------
2. Why are Attributes Important?
-----------------------------------------

- Define what data will be stored.
- Help create database tables.
- Improve data organization.
- Reduce duplicate data.
