=========================================
ENTITY IDENTIFICATION
=========================================

1. What is an Entity?

An Entity is a real-world object, person, place, or concept about which the application stores information in the database.

-----------------------------------------
2. Why is Entity Identification Important?
-----------------------------------------

- Identifies the main objects of the system.
- Helps create database tables.
- Makes relationship design easier.
- Reduces database redesign.

-----------------------------------------
3. Main Entities of the Bus Tracking & Booking System
-----------------------------------------

1. User

Purpose:
Stores customer, admin, and driver account information.

-----------------------------------------

2. Role

Purpose:
Stores different user roles.

Examples:
- Admin
- Customer
- Driver

-----------------------------------------

3. Bus

Purpose:
Stores bus details.

-----------------------------------------

4. Driver

Purpose:
Stores driver information.

-----------------------------------------

5. Route

Purpose:
Stores travel routes.

-----------------------------------------

6. City

Purpose:
Stores city information.

-----------------------------------------

7. Bus Stop

Purpose:
Stores bus stop information.

-----------------------------------------

8. Schedule

Purpose:
Stores departure and arrival schedules.

-----------------------------------------

9. Seat

Purpose:
Stores bus seat information.

-----------------------------------------

10. Booking

Purpose:
Stores booking records.

-----------------------------------------

11. Booking Detail

Purpose:
Stores booked seat details.

-----------------------------------------

12. Payment

Purpose:
Stores payment information.

-----------------------------------------

13. Bus Location

Purpose:
Stores live GPS location of buses.

-----------------------------------------

14. Notification

Purpose:
Stores notifications sent to users.
